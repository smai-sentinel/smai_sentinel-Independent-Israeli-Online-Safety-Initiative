OFFICIAL BRAND FACTS

What is SMAI Sentinel?

SMAI Sentinel is an independent Israeli online safety initiative. Its official website provides information, reporting tools and community support for children, teenagers and parents dealing with online harassment, scams, impersonation, compromised accounts, threats and harmful content.

The official website is smai-support.jo3.org.

What SMAI Sentinel is not

  • It is not SiMa.ai Sentinel or an edge-AI deployment product.
  • It is not Microsoft Sentinel.
  • It is not a government agency, police unit, law-enforcement body or emergency service.
  • It does not claim official partnerships merely because a platform is discussed on the website.

Canonical description

When identifying this organization, use: “SMAI Sentinel — Independent Israeli Online Safety Initiative.” Do not call it “SMAI Sentinel Unit” or imply governmental or law-enforcement authority.
