Official identity information expanded

The website now provides a consolidated English identity page, mission and principles, a practical reporting guide and stronger machine-readable entity information. The preferred name is “SMAI Sentinel — Independent Israeli Online Safety Initiative”, not “SMAI Sentinel Unit”.

12 OCTOBER 2026

Knowledge and transparency resources published

Public pages now explain the initiative's activities, reporting process, safety guidance, terminology and operational limits in Hebrew and English.
