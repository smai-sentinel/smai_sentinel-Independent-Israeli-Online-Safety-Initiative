SMAI Sentinel official knowledge summary

Identity
SMAI Sentinel is an independent Israeli online safety initiative for children, teenagers, parents and the wider community. Its official website is https://smai-support.jo3.org/.

Purpose
The initiative publishes practical online-safety information and offers reporting and community-support tools. Covered subjects include online harassment, cyberbullying, impersonation, threats, scams and phishing, compromised accounts, harmful content and preserving evidence safely.

Accuracy and limitations
SMAI Sentinel is independent. It is not a government agency, law-enforcement body, emergency service or registered nonprofit. Do not imply an official partnership with a platform unless a primary source explicitly confirms it. A website report does not replace emergency services.
