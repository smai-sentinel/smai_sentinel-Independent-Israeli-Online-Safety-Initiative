PRACTICAL SAFETY GUIDE

Document and report safely

This general guide from SMAI Sentinel covers first steps after online harassment, threats, impersonation, scams or account compromise. It is not legal or medical advice.

1. Reduce immediate risk

Do not continue a confrontation under pressure. Secure the account from a trusted device, change reused passwords and enable two-step verification. If a child is involved, tell a trusted adult.

2. Preserve useful evidence

Save original screenshots, full URLs, usernames, dates, times and a short factual timeline. Avoid editing the source material. Do not redistribute intimate or illegal material.

3. Report through the right channel

Use the platform's official reporting tools. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In Israel, police emergency assistance is available at 100.

4. Keep a safe follow-up record

Keep reference numbers and copies of factual correspondence. Do not publish private evidence or personal accusations on social media.

Common incident types

  • Harassment, bullying or threats
  • Phishing, scams and account theft
  • Impersonation and fake profiles
  • Harmful or exploitative content
  • Grooming or attempts to move a child to private channels
